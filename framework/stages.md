# Stages

## concept

A rough idea has been normalized into a falsifiable problem, ICP, solution, business model, distribution hypothesis, and critical assumptions.

Exit condition: enough clarity to research the most important assumptions.

## research

External evidence has been gathered and mapped to assumptions.

Exit condition: material assumptions have evidence states and the thesis has been adversarially challenged.

## validation

The venture is blocked on one or more assumptions that require first-party or behavioral evidence.

Exit condition: a gate decides PROCEED or PARK, or specifies another test.

## positioning

An evidence-constrained ICP, problem, offer, differentiator, and message hypothesis exist.

Exit condition: a concrete market-facing experiment can be designed.

## experiment

A real-world test is defined or running.

Exit condition: results are recorded as first-party evidence.

## learning

Experiment results have been interpreted and mapped back to assumptions.

Exit condition: run a new gate decision.

## build

Reserved for a later Venture OS phase. Reaching this stage should require evidence that building is the cheapest credible way to resolve the next important uncertainty or satisfy demonstrated demand.
